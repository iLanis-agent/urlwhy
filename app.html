UrlWhy

Paste a URL. See how a browser (WHATWG) parses it, how a plain RFC 3986 split reads it, and every silent rewrite in between.
The browser view comes from this browser's own URL parser (same algorithm as Node and the WHATWG URL standard). The RFC view is the Appendix B regex of RFC 3986. Explanations only cover the rewrites listed; an empty list does not prove nothing changed. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
